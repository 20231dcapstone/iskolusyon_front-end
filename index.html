1
Create Account / Login
For New Users, create an account
  • 1Click the Sign Up button on the navigation bar or homepage.
  • 2A Data Privacy Consent screen will appear. Read it carefully and click I Agree to proceed.
  • 3Fill in the registration form with your personal information (full name, date of birth, address, contact number, etc.). Ensure all details are accurate and correct — these will be used for your scholarship application.
  • 4Enter your email address and create a secure password.
  • 5Click Send Verification Code. A one-time code will be sent to your registered email address.
  • 6Enter the verification code in the provided field and click Verify & Create Account. Your account will be created upon successful verification.
For Existing Users, log in
  • 1Click the Log In button on the navigation bar.
  • 2Enter your registered email address and password.
  • 3Click Send Verification Code. A one-time code will be sent to your email for security.
  • 4Enter the code and click Verify & Log In to access your account dashboard.

ⓘ Make sure to use a valid and accessible email address. Check your spam/junk folder if you don't receive the code within a few minutes.

2
Fill Out Your Profile
  • 1After logging in, go to your Dashboard and click Complete My Profile.
  • 2Fill in your Personal Information (full name, date of birth, gender, civil status, and residential address).
  • 3Enter your Academic Information (current or last school attended, grade level, and GWA/GPA).
  • 4Provide your Family & Financial Background (parent/guardian details, household income, and number of siblings).
  • 5Review all entries for accuracy (incorrect information may delay or affect the processing of your application).
  • 6Click Save Profile to store your information.

ⓘ You can update your profile before submitting your application. Once submitted, changes may require revalidation.

3
Upload Required Documents
  • 1From your Dashboard, click Upload Documents.
  • 2Prepare the required documents based on your applicant type (Freshman, Transferee, etc.). Refer to the Admission Requirements tab for the full list.
  • 3Scan or take clear photos of each document. Accepted formats are PDF, JPG, and PNG (max 5MB per file).
  • 4Upload each document in the correct labeled slot (e.g., Report Card, Birth Certificate, Voter's ID).
  • 5Once all documents are uploaded, click Submit Documents.
  • 6You will receive a confirmation notification that your documents are under review.

ⓘ Ensure all documents are clear, complete, and unedited. Blurry or incomplete submissions will be flagged for resubmission.

4
Check Your Qualification
  • 1After document verification is complete, you will receive a qualification status notification via email and in your portal dashboard.
  • 2Log in to your account and go to My Application > Qualification Status.
  • 3Your qualification result will be displayed — either Qualified, Conditionally Qualified, or Not Qualified.
  • 4If conditionally qualified, follow the instructions to submit any missing or corrected documents.
  • 5If qualified, you will be scheduled for the scholarship qualifying exam. Proceed to Step 5.

ⓘ Qualification is based on submitted documents and eligibility criteria. Contact the GCTCDO office if you have questions about your status.

5
Take the Qualifying Exam
  • 1Once qualified, you will receive an exam schedule notification via email and in your portal dashboard.
  • 2Log in on the scheduled exam date and go to My Application > Qualifying Exam.
  • 3Read the exam instructions carefully before starting.
  • 4Complete the exam within the allotted time. Do not close the browser tab during the exam.
  • 5Submit your answers by clicking Submit Exam when done. A confirmation screen will appear.

ⓘ Make sure you have a stable internet connection before starting the exam. Missed exam schedules must be rebooked through the GCTCDO office.

6
Track Your Application Result
  • 1After the exam, your results will be processed by the GCTCDO office.
  • 2Log in to your account and go to My Application > Application Status to check for updates.
  • 3You will also receive real-time email and portal notifications as your application progresses.
  • 4Status updates include: Under Review, For Interview, Scholarship Granted, or Not Selected.
  • 5If granted a scholarship, follow the onboarding instructions sent to your email to complete enrollment.

ⓘ Processing times may vary. For urgent concerns, contact the GCTCDO office directly via email or Facebook Messenger.

No student shall be denied admission to this College by reason of age, sex, nationality, religious belief, or political affiliation. However, all students seeking admission must be subject to the regulations for admission prescribed by the City College of Calamba–Academic Personnel Council.

Academically qualified and deserving applicants must be a resident of the City of Calamba and have graduated from any public or private schools recognized by the Department of Education (DepEd) or are transferees from a tertiary institution recognized by Commission on Higher Education (CHED) with good academic and moral standing.

Residents of the City of Calamba and have a Voter's ID or COMELEC Voter's Certificate. For non-registered voters, a parent or guardian's voter ID will do.

All qualified applicants must submit the following documents to the City College of Calamba for admission evaluation. The following requirements must be submitted to the CCC by the applicants:

First Years

The City College will accept transferees provided they have passed the following qualifications:

  1. The applicant does not have a failing grade in any semester from their previous school/s.
  2. The applicants have passed at least 28 academic units with a general weighted average of 2.25 or better for all collegiate academic units they have taken.
  3. The applicant will have to complete not less than 50% of the remaining units in his/her program at the college.
  4. The Program Director has the prerogative regarding the admission of transferees with exceptional cases, i.e., the same will be dealt with on a case-to-case basis, grades of 5.0, dropped, and INC.

Old Curriculum and ALS Completer

In cases, the prospective student graduated High School on/or before 2017, where K-12 has not been implemented yet, additional requirements need to be satisfied. Admissions
Application Process & Requirements

All official announcements such as qualifications, steps, and exam schedules are posted directly on this website. CCC-granted scholars shall receive specified announcements such as allowances.

Applicants must fill out a step-by-step process from this website after account creation and upload scanned copies of their initial documents.

Applicants must be bonafide residents of Calamba and must have a voter's registration.

There is no minimum grade requirement, but applicants must strictly have no failing grades on their academic record and must pass the entrance exam.

You will only receive an official application number once your documents are identified as complete and valid.

Submitting fake IDs or falsified documents is strictly prohibited and actively monitored by the admissions office.

Examination Details

The exam permit is issued onsite as verification that all requirements have been submitted. A photograph of the applicant will be taken onsite to verify identity.

Schedules are by batch; the first 100 submissions are assigned to the first batch, and subsequent applicants are scheduled for different days.

Failing to attend your scheduled entrance examination will result in the forfeiture of your application. If you have a valid personal reason for missing it, rescheduling can be requested via your account in this website.

Results & Next Steps

Examination results are announced via this website for applicants to review.

Passers are identified as scholars and will be issued a recommendation to proceed with their medical exam and official enrollment.

Registrars

If you are a currently enrolled Senior High School student, you only need to submit photocopies during the admission phase. If you have already graduated or stopped studying, you must present original copies to avoid discrepancies. Original copies will be required for all applicants once they pass the exam and proceed to official enrollment.
